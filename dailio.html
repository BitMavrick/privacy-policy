Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Dailio is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

The short version

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. Your habits, notes, and history stay on your device. Dailio has no accounts, ads, analytics, or third-party trackers, and the app does not request the Android Internet permission.

1. Who we are

Dailio is an Android habit-tracking app developed and published by bitmavrick (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy applies to the Dailio Android app (package name com.bitmavrick.dailio) and the legal pages hosted at this location.

For privacy questions, contact us at support@example.com.

2. Information you create in Dailio

Dailio stores the information you choose to enter so it can provide the app’s features. This may include:

  • habit names, descriptions, icons, colors, categories, schedules, reminders, and checklists;
  • completion records, dates, skip reasons, and optional notes;
  • optional journal notes and mood selections;
  • focus-session history;
  • an optional display name; and
  • app preferences such as theme, week start, day rollover, and notification settings.

This information is stored locally in the app’s private storage on your device, using an on-device database and preferences storage. We do not receive, access, or process this information on our own systems.

Journal notes, moods, and skip notes Your device No

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Dailio does not require an account or sign-in. The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, and it does not request the Android Internet permission. We cannot see your habits, notes, moods, or usage history.

Dailio does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. These legal pages are static HTML and do not set cookies or run analytics.

4. Device permissions

Dailio asks only for permissions needed to support features you choose to use. Each permission is optional, and you can change it in Android Settings.

  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): used to show habit reminders. If you do not allow notifications, reminders will not appear.
  • Exact alarms (Android 12 and later): used to deliver reminders at the time you choose. If exact alarms are unavailable, reminders may arrive later.
  • Run after restart: used to reschedule reminders after your device restarts, updates, or changes time zone.

Dailio does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or general storage. When you create a backup or export a CSV file, Android’s system file picker lets you choose the destination.

5. Reminders and notifications

Habit reminders are scheduled and displayed locally by your device. A notification may show the name of the habit you created. Reminders are not sent through our servers, and we do not track whether you open or act on a notification.

You can turn reminders off in Dailio’s settings or disable notifications for Dailio in Android Settings.

6. Backups, exports, and sharing

Dailio lets you create a JSON backup, export your history as a CSV file, and share a short habit message through Android’s share sheet. These actions happen only when you choose them.

  • Backups and CSV exports are written to a file location you select using Android’s system file picker. We do not receive a copy.
  • Backup and export files are not encrypted by Dailio. Anyone who can access the file or its storage location may be able to read it.
  • When you use the share action, the message is passed to the app you choose. We do not receive the message or know who you shared it with.
  • You are responsible for the files you create and for the people you choose to share them with.

7. Android system backup

Dailio allows Android’s system backup feature. If you have Android backup enabled on your device, your device may back up app data to your Google account. That backup is controlled by your device and Google account settings, not by Dailio, and Google’s privacy policy applies to it.

You can turn off device backups or exclude Dailio in your Android backup settings. If you restore a device from a backup, Android may restore Dailio’s local data as part of that process.

8. Third-party services

Dailio is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about your download and use of the store under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal data from Google Play.

The app uses open-source Android and Kotlin libraries, including AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore, Navigation Compose, and Kotlin serialization. These libraries run on your device and do not send your data to us.

If you follow a link from Dailio or these pages to another service, that service’s own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or apps.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual habits, use Settings → Reset app to erase all app data, clear Dailio’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Dailio removes its local app data from that device.

Backup and CSV files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. Android system backups may remain according to Google’s retention practices and your device backup settings.

Because we do not receive your data, we cannot access, export, or delete it on your behalf. You can export a copy or delete it directly in the app.

10. Security

Dailio stores your data in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Dailio does not add its own encryption to the local database or to backup and export files.

No method of storage is completely secure. We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and storing backup files somewhere you trust. If you share a backup file, the recipient may be able to read its contents.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under privacy laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or the CCPA/CPRA, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to data portability and non-discrimination.

Because we do not receive your data, you can exercise these rights directly in Dailio: view and edit your data, export a backup or CSV file, delete individual habits, or use Reset app to erase everything. If you have questions, contact us at support@example.com.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by applicable privacy laws, and we do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

12. Children’s privacy

Dailio is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us at support@example.com and we will delete it.

13. International users

Dailio stores your data on your device, so we do not transfer your app data internationally. If your device uses Android system backup, Google may process that backup in other countries under its own privacy policy.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version at this location and change the effective date above. If the changes are material, we may also provide notice in the app or in the Google Play listing.

Your continued use of Dailio after the updated policy takes effect means you accept the updated policy.

Privacy Policy

Journey is a local-first journal. Your words belong on your device, not on a server. This page explains what the app stores, which permissions it asks for, and when Android system services are involved.

What stays on your device

Your entries, titles, moods, topics, places, photos, videos, audio, writing statistics, display name, and optional profile photo are stored in Journey’s private app storage on this device.

Journal text and media, including your profile photo, are encrypted at rest with keys held by Android Keystore. A short-lived decrypted copy may be created in the app cache for viewing or playback; it is cleared when the app starts. The readable export is not encrypted.

On-device suggestions, writing prompts, and summaries are generated locally from your writing; they are not sent to a server. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for Journey’s app data.

There is no Journey account and no Journey cloud sync. We do not receive your entries.

  • No account
  • No Journey server
  • No cloud sync
  • No tracking
Privacy by default

We do not operate a server, and we do not collect, sell, or share your journal. Journey is designed to work without sending your entries anywhere.

Permissions

Journey asks only for the permissions needed for a feature you choose. You can deny any permission and keep using the rest of the app.

Camera

Journey opens your camera app only when you choose to take a photo.

Microphone

Journey records audio only when you choose to record a voice note. Recordings stay with the entry on this device.

Location

Journey asks for location only when you choose to attach your current place. You can allow precise or approximate location.

Notifications

Notifications are used only for reminders you enable. They never include your journal text.

Health Connect

Journey reads steps, sleep, and exercise only when you choose to attach activity. It never writes to Health Connect.

Photos

Journey uses Android’s Photo Picker. It does not request broad access to your photo library.

  • Journal lock: uses your device’s screen lock or biometrics. Journey never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Reminders: scheduled locally and rescheduled after a reboot.

You can review or revoke app permissions at any time in Android Settings.

Location, maps & geocoding

When you tap Use my current location, Journey asks Android for a fresh location fix. You can allow precise or approximate location. Journey uses Android’s system geocoder to suggest a nearby place name; the geocoder is provided by Android and may use a network service. The name and coordinates are stored with the entry on this device, and you can edit or remove the name.

Journey does not embed a live map. The Open in Maps button hands the coordinates to your installed maps app. That maps app is a separate product with its own privacy policy and terms.

If you never attach a place, Journey does not request location.

Backups & exports

Journey can create an encrypted backup of your journal. You choose the password. The password is not stored by Journey and cannot be recovered.

A readable export is also available. It is a plain ZIP that anyone with the file can open. Both the encrypted backup and the readable export are started by you.

Keep your backup password safe. If you lose it, the encrypted backup cannot be opened.

No analytics, ads or tracking

Journey contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no Firebase, and no third-party tracking. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

Android’s system geocoder may use a network service to suggest a place name, as described above. Journey itself does not make direct network requests.

Health Connect

Health Connect data is read on this device through the Android Health Connect API. It is not sent to us or to a third party. Journey never writes to Health Connect. You can revoke access in Health Connect settings at any time, and the rest of the journal continues to work.

Children

Journey is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete an individual entry whenever you want.
  • Delete all journal data from Settings, including entries, photos, videos, audio files, and app-created recovery files.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its private storage from this device.
  • Revoke location, microphone, notification, photo, or Health Connect access in Android settings.
  • Encrypted backups and readable exports that you saved outside the app are under your control and must be deleted separately.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. The current version always describes the latest released version of Journey.
